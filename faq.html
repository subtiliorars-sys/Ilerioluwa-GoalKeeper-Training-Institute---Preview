FAQ

Questions parents ask us

Straight answers. Anything not covered here — ask us on WhatsApp and we'll reply directly.

Joining

What ages do you train?

From under-15 up to 18 and above — two tiers: Development (U15–U17) and Senior (18+). See Programs.

Do girls train too?

Yes. Boys and girls train together in mixed sessions, with the same coaching and the same standards.

Is the first session really free?

Yes — completely free for every new keeper, no commitment. It's how you and your keeper find out if we're the right fit. Book it here.

How do I register my keeper?

Registration is always open — there is no fixed intake window. Message us on WhatsApp with your keeper's name and age. Bring a signed consent form to the first session.

Cost

How much does training cost?

U15–U17: ₦10,000/month  ·  18+: ₦15,000/month.
Your first session is always free — no commitment needed. See Programs & Fees for payment details.

Do you offer scholarships?

Yes. Scholarship places exist so that talent, not money, decides who trains. Ask us about them on WhatsApp.

Can my keeper get private coaching?

Yes — one-on-one sessions are available on request, built around the individual keeper. Book on WhatsApp.

Training & safety

Where and when do you train?

Monday, Tuesday, Wednesday · 7:30 AM – 9:00 AM
Marvelous Pitch, Liberty Stadium, Ibadan — see Schedule.

Is my child safe with you?

Our Head Coach is child-protection and safeguarding trained, and parents are welcome to stay and watch every session. Read our full safeguarding & photo policy.

Will my child's photo appear online?

Never without your written permission. We publish no photo in which a child is identifiable unless a parent or guardian has agreed in writing — and you can withdraw that permission at any time.

What should my keeper bring?

Equipment (boots, gloves, jersey) is included — each keeper trains with their own kit. Bring water, shin pads, and a signed consent form on the first session.

Do you run holiday clinics?

Yes — day clinics are available. Message us on WhatsApp for upcoming dates.

How quickly will I see results?

Development becomes visible within 3 months of regular training — parents tell us they notice the difference in their keeper's confidence and technique by that point.

What if my keeper is injured at training?

A trained first-aider is on site at every session. The venue is Marvelous Pitch, Liberty Stadium, and parents are welcome to stay and watch.

Do you speak Yoruba?

Yes — the coaching staff speak both English and Yoruba. Parents and keepers are welcome to communicate in either language.
