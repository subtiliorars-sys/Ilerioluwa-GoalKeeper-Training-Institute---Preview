The curriculum — a periodized training year

Keepers don't improve by random drills. Our year is built from six-week themed blocks, so every keeper covers the full craft of goalkeeping every season:

BlockThemeWhat the keeper masters
1Footwork & handlingSet position, catching shapes, movement patterns
2Diving shapeLow/mid/high dives, safe landing, recovery
3Shot-stoppingReactions, parrying decisions, second saves
41v1s & reaction savesClosing space, spreading, composure
5High balls & crossesJudging flight, claiming, punching, commanding
6DistributionThrows, goal kicks, playing out, starting attacks

Each block ends with an assessed session so parents and keepers see measurable progress.
