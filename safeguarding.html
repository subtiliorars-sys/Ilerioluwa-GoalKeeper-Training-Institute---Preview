Safeguarding

Your child's safety comes before football

A young keeper can't grow without trust — yours and theirs. This page says plainly how we protect the keepers in our care, on the pitch and online.

On the pitch

🛡️ Safeguarding-trained coaching

Our Head Coach is child-protection and safeguarding trained — and you don't have to take our word for how training is run: come and watch any session, any time.

👀 Parents are always welcome

You may stay and watch every session, every time, without asking. We'd rather you did — keepers love an audience, and you should see exactly what your child's training looks like.

Online — our photo & video policy

📵 No permission, no photo

We publish no photo or video in which a child is identifiable — on this website or on our social media — unless a parent or guardian has agreed in writing first. No exceptions, including for our own promotional material.

🙅 Names stay private

Even with photo permission, we never publish a child's full name, school, or home area alongside their image unless you separately agree in writing.

↩️ You can change your mind

Permission isn't forever. Message us on WhatsApp at any time and we will take your child's images down promptly — our target is within 48 hours.

⚖️ Saying no costs nothing

Declining photos — or withdrawing later — changes nothing about your child's place, playing time, or treatment at the academy. Ever.

If something concerns you

If anything at training ever worries you or your keeper — anything at all — contact us directly and we will take it seriously: WhatsApp +234 703 619 0935 or akindekotobi3@gmail.com.
