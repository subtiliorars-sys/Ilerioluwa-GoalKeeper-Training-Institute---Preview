Trials & Registration

Your first session is free

Every keeper trains with us once before paying anything. Come, train, meet the coaches — then decide.

Book FREE trial on WhatsApp

How it works

  1. Message us on WhatsApp with the keeper's name, age, and preferred location. We confirm your free trial slot.
  2. Attend the free session. Bring boots, gloves if you have them (we have loan pairs), water, and shin pads.
  3. Get an honest assessment. The coach recommends a program tier and discusses goals with the parent/guardian.
  4. Register and pay securely online. Complete the short registration and pay the first month via our secure payment link.

What to bring

Required

  • Football boots (or trainers for first session)
  • Water bottle
  • Shin pads
  • Parent/guardian contact details

Good to have

  • Goalkeeper gloves (loan pairs available)
  • Long-sleeved training top
  • Any medical information we should know

Frequently asked

Is the first session really free? Yes — every keeper trials before committing. No card, no deposit.

My child has never played in goal. Perfect. The U8–U10 Foundations tier starts from zero.

Do you take girls? Yes — girls' programs run across all tiers.

What if we can't afford fees? Ask about scholarships at your trial — talent is never priced out of the goal.
