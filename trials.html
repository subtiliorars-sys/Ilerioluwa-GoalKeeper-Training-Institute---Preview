Your first session is free

Every keeper trains with us once before paying anything. Come, train, meet the coaches — then decide.

How it works

  1. Message us on WhatsApp with the keeper's name, age, and preferred location. We confirm your free trial slot.
  2. Attend the free session. Bring water and shin pads — and message us about boots and gloves before you come, we'll tell you exactly what's needed.
  3. Get an honest assessment. The coach recommends a program tier and discusses goals with the parent/guardian.
  4. Register and pay. Pay the first month by bank transfer to Akindeko Oluwatobiloba Simon · 1485496863 · Access Bank, then send your receipt on WhatsApp to confirm your place.

What to bring

Required

  • Signed consent form (template on request — message us on WhatsApp)
  • Water bottle
  • Shin pads
  • Parent/guardian contact details

Provided by the academy

  • Training equipment — each keeper trains with their own boots, gloves, and jersey
  • A trained first-aider is on site at every session
  • Full training session — every keeper trains the complete session, not partial

Frequently asked

Is the first session really free? Yes — every keeper trials before committing. No card, no deposit.

My son/daughter has never played in goal. Perfect. We start from the fundamentals — no experience needed.

Do you take girls? Yes — boys and girls train together across all sessions.

What are the monthly fees? U15–U17: ₦10,000/month. 18+: ₦15,000/month. See Programs & Fees for payment details.

When does registration open? Registration is always open — no fixed intake window. Book your free trial any time on WhatsApp.
